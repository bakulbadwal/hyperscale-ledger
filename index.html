Reference instrument · GBUS 8255 Business of AI · Session 3

Audited NVIDIA datacenter GPU corpus, and a 2027 hyperscale scenario model built on it.

NVIDIA's headline tensor figures are footnoted “with sparsity” and are exactly twice dense; the H100's sparse TF32 and dense BF16 are both 989 TFLOPS; SXM and PCIe parts of one generation carry different TDPs; and rack power is published by nobody. This corpus keeps those qualifiers as structure — sparse and dense are separate fields, variants are separate records, unpublished values stay empty.

Huang's Law, re-derived with precision held fixed

Flagship SXM parts only · dense FP16/BF16 only · the one basis every generation from Pascal to Blackwell publishes.

Why this matters. Published “GPU performance over time” curves usually plot each generation's headline number. But the headline format keeps narrowing — FP16, then FP8, then FP4 — and each halving of precision roughly doubles the number without the silicon doing more work. Comparing Blackwell FP4 to Pascal FP16 measures the arrival of new number formats as much as it measures hardware. Holding precision fixed is the only honest version of this chart.
